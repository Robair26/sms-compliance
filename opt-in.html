SMS Notifications Opt-In

Members of St. Mark Coptic Orthodox Church of Los Angeles may voluntarily opt in to receive SMS notifications related to church services, volunteer scheduling, and church activity reminders.

How Members Opt In

Church members and volunteers provide their mobile phone number through a church volunteer registration form or church communication sign-up form managed by church coordinators.

Consent Language Used During Sign-Up

“By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS notifications from St. Mark Coptic Orthodox Church of Los Angeles regarding church service schedules, volunteer coordination, and church activity reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Consent is not required for participation in church activities.”
